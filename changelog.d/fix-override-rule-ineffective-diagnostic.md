### Fixed

- **An `overrides` rule that cannot change a result now reports a diagnostic.** Fallow reads only the top-level severity of a project-level rule, so an override of `dev-dependencies-in-production` for `tools/**` did nothing and gave no message. The new `override-rule-ineffective` entry in `workspace_diagnostics[]` carries `rule`, `files` and `reason`, and its message names the mechanism that works. `reason: project-level` covers the project-level rules. `reason: base-rule-off` covers an override of `warn` or `error` for a rule that is off at top level, because the detector then does not run. Findings and exit codes do not change. SARIF, Markdown, the PR comments, the review body, `fallow report --from`, the stderr note and the LSP show the same entries. The old stderr warnings for `duplicate-exports` and `re-export-cycle` moved to this entry. They no longer print in `health` and `dupes`, or with `--quiet` in human output. The `overrides` schema text no longer says that `circular-dependencies` has no effect. Thanks @Smrtnyk for the report.
  (Closes [#4065](https://github.com/fallow-rs/fallow/issues/4065))
