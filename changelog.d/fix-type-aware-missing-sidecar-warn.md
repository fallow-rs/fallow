### Fixed

- **A missing type-aware sidecar no longer fails the gate on findings that the type-aware pass can disprove.** With `typeAware.enabled: true`, `typeAware.require: best-effort` (the default), and no `fallow-type-aware` sidecar, `dead-code`, `audit` and the bare run report `unused-export`, `unused-type`, `unused-class-member` and `private-type-leak` findings at `warn`, also under `--fail-on-issues` and `--ci`. Every other `warn` rule is still raised to `error` by these flags. Before, a Homebrew or `cargo install` binary reported these findings at `error` and exited 1, for example on a class method that only the type-aware pass sees as used. `_meta.type_aware.executed` stays `false`, the warning stays, and `gate_outcomes` gets a `type-aware-fallback` entry with `status: skipped`. A sidecar that starts and then fails or times out keeps the configured severities, and `require: complete` still exits 2. (Closes [#4606](https://github.com/fallow-rs/fallow/issues/4606))
